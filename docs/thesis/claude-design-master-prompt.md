# Claude Design Master Prompt

Create a polished, functional React application called **Sayina HAN Thesis Command Center** for a HAN graduation thesis about FOMO-based social-media marketing and Generation Z purchase intentions toward international fashion brands.

Use a calm European academic aesthetic: white/light-grey surfaces, deep navy, a muted accent, accessible contrast, responsive desktop/tablet layout, and restrained motion. Do not use the official HAN logo unless an authorized asset is supplied.

Include: Executive Dashboard; RC1–RC6 workspace; Research Question alignment checker; Theory and Conceptual Model; searchable Literature Library; Research Method and operationalization matrix; Data and Results; Gap Analysis; evidence-linked Recommendations; provisional FOMO Campaign Ethics and Effectiveness Scorecard; Defense Center; and Risks, Decisions, Assumptions, Feedback, and Change History.

Functional requirements: editable records; add/edit/delete; localStorage; JSON import/export; search/sort/filter; calculated progress; empty states; delete confirmation; accessible responsive UI. Seed only the approved central question, RC1–RC6, Theory of Planned Behavior, Consumer Decision-Making Process, and the four FOMO dimensions. Mark every sample task and every demo record as a template or DEMO DATA.

Academic safeguards: never fabricate citations or findings; unverified sources must remain unverified; charts may use approved real data only; recommendations cannot be marked evidence-based without links to at least one authentic finding and one documented gap; include checks for APA 7, validity, reliability, ethics, consent, anonymization, limitations, and full research alignment.

Version 1 has no backend or authentication. Prepare a clean entity schema for later Notion or Supabase synchronization.
