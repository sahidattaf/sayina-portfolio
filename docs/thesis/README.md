# Sayina HAN Thesis Command Center

This package adds a functional thesis-control route to Sayina's existing React/Vite portfolio.

## Research scope

**Topic:** FOMO in social-media marketing and its influence on Generation Z purchase intentions toward international fashion brands.

**Central question:** How does Fear of Missing Out (FOMO) in social media marketing influence the purchase intentions of Generation Z consumers toward international fashion brands?

**Primary framework:** Theory of Planned Behavior (attitude, subjective norms, perceived behavioural control, purchase intention).

**Supporting framework:** Consumer Decision-Making Process.

**FOMO dimensions:** scarcity, urgency, exclusivity, and social proof.

## HAN structure

The command center organizes work through RC1–RC6: Topic & Scope; International Business Relevance; Questions, Literature & Models; Methods; Results & Gap Analysis; Recommendations & Reflection.

## Files

- `src/pages/ThesisCommandCenter.jsx` — interactive dashboard at `/thesis`
- `src/data/thesis.js` — versioned seed content

- `docs/thesis/claude-design-master-prompt.md` — prompt for a future full Claude Design artifact

## Privacy and academic integrity

- Never commit identifiable participant data.
- Never commit restricted HAN course files without permission.
- Never invent sources, citations, findings, DOI numbers, or statistics.
- Label templates and demo data explicitly.
- Recommendations must trace to real findings and a documented gap.
